Authored in scripts/prepare-audio.py; two rising notes with soft attack/decay.

Changes: selected excerpts; mono 44.1kHz 16-bit WAV; silence trimming;
55Hz high-pass; peak balance; short fade edges. Game playback layers recordings
with material-specific levels, small rate variations, stereo direction and wall filtering.

manifest.json records the original file, source, edits, hashes, duration and levels
for every bundled audio file. scripts/prepare-audio.py rebuilds these assets from
extracted source downloads with ffmpeg; no download or ffmpeg is needed to play.
