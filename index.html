    The world keeps running in menus and pauses when the last player disconnects. Leave the world to pause single player.

    Click or tap to use menus. Tab and Shift+Tab do not move through controls, and buttons do not keep focus. Click a text field to type.

    Move with the left stick. Drag the world to look. Aim the crosshair, then tap Break or Place. Tap Drop to drop one item from the selected hotbar slot. Tap Menu to return here, then Inventory to move stacks or drop a whole stack. Landscape gives you more room.
